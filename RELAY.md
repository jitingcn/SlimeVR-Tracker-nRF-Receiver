# Peer relay

The receiver uses normal direct TDMA while links are healthy. `relay on` permits automatic relay entry after sustained loss or an outage. It does not start control broadcasts or helper listening by itself. Stable direct recovery stages a coordinated return to direct TDMA. `relay off` withdraws permission and stages the same safe exit; control frames continue until activation so trackers retain their direct clock anchors.

Validated power-down notices provide bounded intent, not proof that a tracker is asleep. Automatic relay decisions exclude that intent while ordinary RF membership and inactivity expiry remain intact. Planned shutdowns cannot acquire or retain the special query-recovery lease. Cancellation, wake, session replacement and pairing invalidation clear the intent; repeated notices do not renew its budget. Unannounced silence still follows the normal outage policy.

An engaged receiver broadcasts control feedback, assigns helper watch windows, and grants repair transmissions. Helpers cache peer packets and transmit only granted repairs. Static cache ages remain 0 through 2; demand scheduling retains its bounded five-frame cache. Helper qualification remains 950 permille. Control planning may prepare the next known frame while the live RX timeline remains on the current frame.

Each control transmission has a four-RTC-tick arming guard after the preceding direct or repair area. The receipt freeze stays one tick before that area's end; hardware launch moves to the end of the guard. The callback is scheduled seven ticks before launch, and compare admission requires at least twelve ticks before the preamble. Direct capture cutoffs, RX lead, admission reserves and R0+3 remain intact. Native profile 0 does not use these guards.

PONG trailers coordinate layout activation and passive clock presence. Profile 0 means normal direct TDMA; profile 1 means engaged relay. Protocol version 5 validates the guarded wire timing. Both firmware repositories carry the same `src/connection/relay_protocol.h`. Update all relay trackers and their receiver together; do not mix these images with the earlier timing.

Permission is `relay_user_enabled`, effective permission is `relay_coord.enabled`, automatic demand is `auto_engaged`, and the applied state is `relay_profile`. Commands are `relay on`, `relay off`, `relay` for status, and `relay layout` for settings. The separate `relay diag` command reports the protocol, power-down intent, member last-seen and valid PING information, query state, owner progress and stage-specific failures. `arm_late` counts missed hardware arm deadlines; `prepare_late` counts insufficient compare-setup lead. The last failed arm retains its frame, repeat flag, freeze-entry lag, error and signed launch margins before and after arming, even after later success. Positive margins mean ticks remaining. Diagnostic block and pool commands remain available. There is no second protocol switch or command alias.

A confirmed `QUERY_INFO` temporarily requires native TDMA. During that query-induced suspension, only members active and unexpired when the suspension began retain their membership lease through staged relay re-entry. They get one 15-second recovery window after activation; an actual direct or repaired receipt restores the ordinary inactivity lease. If the owner misses the entire hold while profile 1 remains applied and no native exit is staged, recovery instead starts once at the next owner merge or query update while relay remains engaged and ready. An already staged native exit still waits for actual relay re-entry. Repeated queries extend the suspension without adding new members. Forgetting a slot, withdrawing relay permission, or losing the ability to re-enter releases protection; normal native mode keeps its usual 15-second inactivity cleanup.

Receiver counters use the same definitions in both profiles. `arrivals` counts validated pose records before deduplication; `duplicate_stale` counts duplicate or stale records rejected at publication; `direct_accepted` and `repair_accepted` count unique accepted records by path. Total TPS counts accepted unique records, including info and status records. Malformed packets and injected direct drops do not enter arrivals. Host `total_received`, gap and status counters retain publication accounting.

Host tests exercise normal links, sustained/intermittent loss, outages, helper selection, bounded caches, repair grants, recovery hold, permission off, staged exit, native sequence re-anchor, clock handoff, control windows, delivery confirmation history and maintenance starvation. Firmware builds include nRF52840, nRF52833 and relay disabled. Host logic tests do not prove RF delivery, physical clock drift or power savings; those require hardware validation.
